# Attribution

Little Atlas city packs mix several sources. They are not one licence.

| Source | Licence | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| OpenStreetMap contributors | [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) | Places, coordinates, names, and tags derived from OSM. <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright> |
| Wikidata | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Identifiers and structured facts |
| GeoNames | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Population and some names. <https://www.geonames.org/> |
| Photographers on Wikimedia Commons | The licence on that file | Each photo. The per-city `*-attribution.json` on the `packs` release names the author, licence, licence URL, and source URL |

Photos are resized and converted to WebP. No other change is made. A file with no machine-readable author, or an author that is only "Own work", is not shipped.

Open-data nursing-room lists keep the licence of that city dataset and are named in the pack's `credits` when a city is published.
